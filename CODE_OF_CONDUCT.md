# Code of conduct

This applies everywhere Lpdf is discussed on GitHub: issues, Discussions and
comments in every lpdfio repository.

## Expected

- Be respectful, and assume good intent.
- Keep to the topic: Lpdf, and how to use it.
- Criticise work, not people.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or imagery
- Posting someone else's private information
- Sexual content
- Spam and advertising

## What happens

Codesense moderates these spaces. We may edit, hide or delete content, lock a
conversation, or block an account, at our discretion.

## Reporting

Use GitHub's **Report content** on the comment, or email
[support@lpdf.io](mailto:support@lpdf.io). Reports are handled privately.
